Blanket math

Every weighted blanket page shouts "10% of your body weight" and then offers six sizes that are none of that number. Blanket math turns your weight into the labeled target, its honest band and the nearest catalog size, settles whether two sleepers share one blanket or sleep better with two singles, and checks the blanket against the washer drum before the first wash wrecks the machine. Labeled rules of thumb, exact arithmetic.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Weight plan - sleeper weight becomes the 10% target, its band and the nearest catalog size.
  • Share plan - two sleepers become a shared target and a share-versus-singles call.
  • Wash plan - blanket against washer drum: easy, tight or laundromat.

Labeled norms

Labeled single rule: about 10% of body weight, inside a 7-12% band. Labeled shared rule: 7.5% of combined weight, and more than 30 kg between sleepers argues for two singles. These are common rules of thumb, not medical advice - weighted blankets are not for infants, and children or anyone who cannot push the blanket off alone need a clinician's or caregiver's call.
